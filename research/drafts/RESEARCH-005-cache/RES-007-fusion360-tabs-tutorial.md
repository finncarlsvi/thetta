**Type**: Community
**Status**: CACHED

## Content
- Tabs are technological features used in CAM machining to hold parts during cutting
- **Tab Shapes**: Rectangular (straight-sided, consistent support) and Triangular (tapered, less material to remove)
- **Positioning Methods**:
  - By Distance: specify spacing between successive tabs via "Tab Distance" parameter
  - At Points: manually designate specific locations
- **Parameters**: Tab Width, Tab Height, Tab Distance
- During machining, cutting tool deliberately raises slightly at tab locations, leaving thin material layers
- Operators later remove tabs manually
