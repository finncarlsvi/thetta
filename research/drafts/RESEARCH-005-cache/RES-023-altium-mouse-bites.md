**Status**: CACHED

## Content
### Mouse Bites
- Small cutout regions and holes between boards in panel
- Detach by hand, file away leftover material
- Design rules: place holes tangent to PCB edge, large diameter, hole-wall spacing < hole diameter
- Routing area: 1.5-2mm around holes
- Tangential positioning minimizes board intrusion

### V-Score Grooves
- Narrow cut lines allowing hand separation or rolling blade
- Need large clearance from score region to nearby copper
- No components near score regions
- Must document score angle, depth, remaining thickness

### Panel Design
- Verify fabricator stocks materials in panel size
- Add tooling holes and fiducials to corners
- Include test coupons for impedance testing
