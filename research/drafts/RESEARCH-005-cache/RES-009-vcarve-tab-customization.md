## From Earlier Search Results
- Tabs can be specified automatically: enter min/max number of tabs + distance between tabs
- Or manually by clicking on profile with mouse
- Even automatic tabs can be repositioned by dragging or click-to-delete/click-to-add
- 3D Tabs: tool makes smooth 3D move up to tab thickness and back, instead of stop/retract/move/plunge
- 3D tabs give smoother profiling passes, easier to remove with less marking
- For through-cuts, assigning tabs is important to keep parts from moving
- Tab sizes set on Toolpath screen
