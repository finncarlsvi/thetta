# RES-003: Cutting path optimization in CNC cutting processes using a two-step genetic algorithm
**URL**: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/00207540600579615
**Type**: Paper
**Status**: FETCH_FAILED (403 paywall)
**Published**: International Journal of Production Research, Vol 44, No 24

## From Search Results
Deals with generating 2D cutting paths for a stock plate nested with parts. The objective is to minimize the total non-productive traveling distance of a cutter. A cutting path consists of the depot and piercing points, and the problem is formulated as a generalized version of the standard traveling salesman problem. A two-step genetic algorithm combining global search for piercing point optimization and local search for part sequencing is proposed.
