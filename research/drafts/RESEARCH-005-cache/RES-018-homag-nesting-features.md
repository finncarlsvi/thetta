# RES-018: HOMAG Stay-Down, Common Line, Tabs, Onion Skinning
**URL**: https://docs.homag.cloud/en/news/article/stay-down-common-line-include-tabs-onion-skinning
**Type**: Commercial
**Status**: CACHED

## Content
### Stay-Down (Repositioning Without Retraction)
- Tool approaches once and remains at milling depth for entire cycle
- Eliminates individual approach/departure per workpiece
- Significantly reduces machining time

### Common Line (Common Milling Tracks)
- Part spacing matches tool diameter exactly
- Cutter passes between two workpieces once, separating them simultaneously

### Include Tabs (Retaining Bars)
- For small parts prone to shifting due to insufficient vacuum
- intelliDivide Nesting connects small parts with retaining bars
- Tab size and quantity customizable
- After nesting: break off bars or handsaw-separate

### Onion Skinning (Milling in Steps)
- Multi-pass: pre-mill small parts first pass, complete all parts second pass

### Integration
- All four functions work together in Formatting macro
- Requires woodWOP 8 + Nesting Plugin (tabs available v8.1.225+)
