# RES-022: PCB Tab Routing Masterclass (AllPCB)

**Type**: Community
**Status**: CACHED

## Content
### Placement Strategy
- Place tabs away from high-stress areas or corners
- Distribute evenly along board edges, one every 50-75mm
- 2-3mm edge clearance from components/traces
- 3-5 tabs per side for standard boards

### Dimensions
- Tab width: 0.2-0.5mm for standard 1.6mm FR-4
- For larger boards (>200mm): add one tab per 50mm of edge length

### Mouse Bites
- Hole diameters: 0.5-1.0mm, spaced 1.0mm apart
- 5-7 holes per tab for standard 1.6mm FR-4
- Perforations distribute force evenly during separation, reducing crack risk

### Material Considerations
- FR-4 tensile strength ~310 MPa supports standard tab designs
- Brittle materials (ceramics): reduce tab width or increase perforation holes

### Simulation
- Simulation software can predict stress points and guide placement
