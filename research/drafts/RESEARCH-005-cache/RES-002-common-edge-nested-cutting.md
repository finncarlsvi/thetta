## From Search Results
Different from normal nesting algorithms, common-edge nesting reduces the spacing between parts, improves sheet utilization rate, and optimizes cutting paths. The paper addresses optimizing cutting sequences and paths specifically for parts that share common edges.

## Relevance
Directly relevant to the shared-tab problem: when parts share common edges, tab placement must account for the shared cutting line.
