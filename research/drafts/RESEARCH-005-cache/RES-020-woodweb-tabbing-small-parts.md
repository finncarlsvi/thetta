# RES-020: Tabbing Versus Onion-Skinning for Small Parts

**Type**: Community
**Status**: CACHED

## Content
### When to Use Tabs
- Stronger hold-down, especially thick tabs (1/4" thick "totally locks everything")
- Requires additional sanding time

### When to Use Onion-Skinning
- Two-stage: cut to 0.70 in 0.75 sheet, then full path for balance
- Full-sheet approach works with optimization

### Practical Tips
- Reduce effective table size (cover unused spoilboard with cardboard) to concentrate vacuum
- Ensure sheets lie flat (warped material = poor vacuum)
- Cut small parts first when vacuum strongest
- Use sharp compression bits for large parts, down-shear bits for small
- Two-pass method reduces chip accumulation and movement
