# RES-025: Workholding Best Practices for Tabbing (Mate)
**URL**: https://www.mate.com/workholding-best-practices-for-tabbing/
**Type**: Commercial
**Status**: CACHED

## Content
- Tabbing: 5-axis strategy where all six sides machined in single operation
- Workpiece remains attached by small tab until completion

### Four Critical Factors
1. **Tab Location**: connects to face requiring no machining; if all faces need machining, must identify one that can accommodate tab
2. **CAM Software Support**: must natively support tabbing strategies
3. **Workholding Setup**: maximize clearance with elevated components (DynoMount risers, chamfered vise designs)
4. **Tab Geometry**: thick enough for rigidity, thin enough for easy removal
   - Smaller/harder workpieces + lower forces → thinner tabs
   - Larger/softer workpieces + higher forces → thicker tabs
