## From Search Results
- At least 3 tabs recommended, can be small and thin at final cuts
- A scrap island with only 2 tabs could rotate — minimum 3-4 tabs
- Tabs should be on straight edges or outside curves for easy sanding access
- Simple manual approach: divide perimeter into 3 where ends don't meet
- Common setup: tabs 0.75" long × 0.125" high
- Size depends on specific project
