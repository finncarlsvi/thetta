## From Search Results
Classification and mathematical models of tool path problems are considered, formalizing cost/time minimization tasks for various cutting techniques. The formalization allows consideration of thermal cutting constraints, and in some cases the optimization tasks can be interpreted as discrete optimization problems (generalized travel salesman problem with additional constraints, GTSP).
