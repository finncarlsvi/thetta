## From Search Results and Metadata
- Bridges & Tabs are areas where stock material by design is allowed to touch the part, forming bridges between the part and remaining stock
- Once machining is complete, bridges are removed manually (cut, ground, or sanded)
- In MecSoft's Mill module, Bridges & Tabs can be defined from Advanced Cut Parameters tab of 2½ Axis Profiling operation dialog
- Bridge type: Triangular or Rectangular
- Parameters: Bridge Height, Bridge Length, Number of Bridges or Distance between Bridges
- Automatic mode: when operation is generated, tabs are added to toolpath automatically
- Manual mode: Bridge Points placed on a Curve Region with Add/Edit/Delete controls
